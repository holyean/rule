# Advertising rule sources and attribution

This project downloads third-party advertising rules and transforms them by normalization,
network-rule selection, deduplication, CIDR collapse, and GitHub-domain exclusion.
Upstream authors retain their rights. This notice does not relicense the combined dataset
or the rest of the repository. Source URLs and hashes are recorded in the build report.
